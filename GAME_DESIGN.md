# Game Design

## Vision

Shepperton Life RPG is a friendly, slightly humorous life game about building a better routine in a compact English village. It is approachable to people who do not already know RPG terminology.

The map is a fictional, compressed interpretation created for play. It contains a high street, residential edges, station, green, riverside boundary and useful locations placed within short walking distances.

## Core loop

1. Wake at Rosehip Court.
2. Check cash, bank balance, health, energy, time and the current objective.
3. Walk into the village and choose useful actions.
4. Work for cash and reputation.
5. Study, train or socialise to improve attributes.
6. Buy and use items; move spare cash into the bank.
7. Return home, save, upgrade when ready and sleep.

Movement does not advance the clock in milestone one. Meaningful actions advance time by visible fixed amounts. This keeps touch exploration relaxed and makes transactions deterministic.

## Starting rules

- Strength, Intelligence and Charm begin at 1.
- The player spends exactly 10 additional points.
- No starting attribute can exceed 8.
- Starting cash is £40, with a possible £5 background bonus.
- Day 1 begins Monday at 08:00.
- Strength adds two maximum health per point above 1.

Backgrounds provide small, explicit effects: cash/reputation or maximum energy. There is no dice roll.

## Progression

- Café Assistant is available immediately.
- Café Shift Leader needs Charm 4 and Reputation 4.
- Office Trainee needs Intelligence 3 and Charm 2.
- Office Administrator needs Intelligence 6 and Reputation 6.
- Lockside Studio needs Reputation 5.
- The first home upgrade costs £120 and needs Reputation 2.

The first-day objective sequence teaches exploration, work, buying food, self-improvement, returning home and sleeping without blocking free play.

## Location presentation

Milestone one uses purpose-built HTML location panels over a paused world. This supports reliable iPad touch targets, scrollable content, clear costs and safe atomic actions while leaving the map visible as context.
