# Roadmap

## Milestone 1 — Village life vertical slice

Title, character setup, exploration, touch controls, jobs, progression, shopping, banking, objectives, home/sleep and local saving. This is the current implementation.

## Milestone 2 — People and stories

- Original NPCs with schedules.
- Dialogue, relationships and short village quests.
- More qualifications and job branches.
- Small playable interiors where they improve the experience.
- A weather layer and simple day/night lighting.

## Milestone 3 — Home and economy

- Property choices, furniture placement and recurring bills.
- Savings interest and carefully enforced loans.
- More shops, item effects and second-hand trading with safe resale rules.
- Achievements and longer objective chains.

## Milestone 4 — Wider Shepperton

- A larger properly licensed geography layer, if suitable source data is supplied.
- Bus, rail and bicycle travel.
- Additional districts, careers, events and minigames.

## Later technical options

Cloud saves, accounts and cross-device play require a deliberately selected backend, privacy policy and migration plan. They are not implied by the local-save milestone.
